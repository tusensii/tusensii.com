No. V

Gadwall

Mareca strepera
Locality
Washington Park Arboretum
Season
Summer
Behavior
Kleptoparasitism

You might look at this and think, "ahh, the common female Mallard," and you would be WRONG. This is the Gadwall, which steals the food of the Mallard as soon as it returns from diving. Look for the fine vermiculation vs. larger brown patterning.

No. IX

Yellow
Warbler

Setophaga petechia
Locality
Nisqually National Wildlife Refuge
Season
Spring
Migration
They have one of the broadest breeding ranges of any warbler, from Alaska across nearly all of North America down into Mexico, with mangrove-dwelling subspecies in Central America and the Caribbean.

The Brown-headed Cowbird will sometimes lay eggs in the Yellow Warbler's nest. Instead of raising the chicks, the Yellow Warbler will occasionally build a new nest on top and lay new eggs. One nest was found with six layers.
